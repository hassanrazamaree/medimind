First-Aid Basics

Only if trained: call 1122 first. If the person is unresponsive and not breathing normally, push hard and fast in the center of the chest — about 100–120 compressions per minute. Continue until help arrives.
Encourage the person to cough. If they cannot breathe or speak, stand behind them and give firm abdominal thrusts (Heimlich maneuver) until the object comes out or help arrives.
Apply firm, direct pressure on the wound with a clean cloth or bandage. Keep pressing and call 1122 immediately if bleeding does not stop. Do not remove soaked bandages — add more on top.
Cool the burn under clean, running water for at least 20 minutes. Do not apply ice, toothpaste, or creams. Cover loosely with cling film or a clean cloth and seek medical help for serious burns.

⚠️ These are basic guidance only. This app does not replace professional medical help. In any emergency, call 1122 first.
